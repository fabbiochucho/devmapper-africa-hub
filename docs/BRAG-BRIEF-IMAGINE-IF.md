# DevMapper Launch Video — Brief B: "Imagine If" (preferred)

For use with `/brag` or `/brag-slim`.

## Plot

Open by speaking straight to the four people who actually do this work — a
citizen, an NGO, a government official, a company rep — each already
tracking their own projects somewhere, none of it talking to each other.
The pitch is second-person and aspirational: imagine if you could track,
verify, and report it all in one place. Then show that place.

**All numbers used in this brief are illustrative placeholders, not pulled
live** — this is an invitation to build toward something, not a scoreboard
of what's already proven. (Contrast with Brief A, which uses real pulled
numbers for its proof-at-scale close.)

**Tone:** Same institutional confidence as Brief A, but warmer and more
direct — "you," not "they." No stock-footage clichés — every visual is the
real product.

**Structural device:** Open on 4 roles, close on 4 verbs. The video opens
naming the four audiences and closes on the four actions
(Capture/Verify/Score/Act) — a deliberate rhyme so the piece feels designed,
not just a feature list.

**Length:** ~20 seconds, 7 shots.

**Why the rhyme works:** opening on four people and closing on four verbs
gives the middle section (the feature tour) a reason to exist beyond "look
at our features" — it answers the question the open poses.

**Render note:** shot 6's numbers should stay round and illustrative on
purpose ("thousands," not an exact count) so nothing in the video makes a
claim that needs an audit trail. Do not substitute live-pulled figures here
— that's what distinguishes this brief from Brief A.
