# DevMapper Launch Video — Brief A: "Proof at Scale"

For use with `/brag` or `/brag-slim`.

## Plot

Africa doesn't have a funding problem — it has a trust problem. Billions get
poured into development every year, and most of that impact is never
verified. DevMapper is the infrastructure that turns "we did something" into
"we proved it." The arc: expose the trust gap → show the fix in motion →
prove it's already working at scale → close on urgency.

**Tone:** Institutional confidence, not startup hype. Serious, a little
urgent, ultimately hopeful — matches the app's own voice ("If impact cannot
be verified, it cannot be scaled"). No stock-footage clichés — every visual
is the real product.

**Audience:** NGOs, governments, corporates, funders — people who need to
trust the data before they move money or policy on it.

**Length:** ~20 seconds, 7 shots.

**Why this shape:** Shots 1–2 are the hook (problem, no product yet). Shot 3
is the turn. Shots 4–5 show breadth fast, as rhythm rather than explanation
— DevMapper has too many modules (ESG, PM, Forum, multi-language, Change
Makers) to explain any one in a 20-second cut. Shots 6–7 are proof and close.

**Deliberately cut:** no voiceover walkthrough of how verification actually
works, no admin/back-office screens, no onboarding flow.

**Render note:** shot 6 needs real numbers pulled from the live database at
render time (countries reached, verified reports, funds tracked) — don't
ship this shot with placeholder figures; it's the one place in this brief
where the claim needs to be literally true.
